# MW Voice PRD

## Problem
Service contractors miss calls constantly. Every missed call is a lost job.

## Solution
AI voice agent that answers 24/7, qualifies the lead, books the appointment.

## Target customer
Solo and small service contractors: plumbers, roofers, painters, HVAC, landscaping.
Revenue \$200k–\$2M. Too busy to answer every call, too small for a receptionist.

## Deployment model
SaaS. MW hosts, contractor gets a forwarding number. No install.

## Key decisions
- [ ] Voice provider: ElevenLabs vs Vapi vs Retell
- [ ] CRM: how does it hand off to contractor?
- [ ] Pricing: per-minute vs flat monthly
- [ ] Onboarding: how does contractor configure their business info?
